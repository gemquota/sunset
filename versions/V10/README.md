## Reading map

1. **Nested temporal problem** — the refrain as nested time.
2. **Nesting without determinism** — historical connection without inevitable technological destiny.
3. **Form enacts subject** — repetition, temporal nesting, and retrospective reinterpretation.
4. **Recursive listener** — the interpreter becomes part of the operative structure.
5. **Recursive interpretation / RSI** — cognition and rereading as the strongest bridge.
6. **Representation / experience** — records versus the experiences they represent.
7. **Words are stupid** — limits of representation.
8. **Cosmic epitaph** — humanity's archive after humanity.
9. **Version archive** — evidence of recursive interpretation, not automatic evidence of correctness.
10. **Competing interpretations** — existential, technological, epistemological, recursive-form, and hybrid readings.
11. **CIITV** — explicit methodological self-correction.

V11 extends this architecture by making the future machine interpreter explicitly open-ended rather than treating AGI or ASI as an assumed endpoint.
