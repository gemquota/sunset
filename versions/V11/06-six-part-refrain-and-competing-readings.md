# 06 — Six-Part Refrain and Competing Readings

The six-part model remains a structured interpretive model rather than a decoding key:

1. biological evolution;
2. technological development;
3. possible machine intelligence;
4. information versus experience;
5. irreversibility;
6. cosmic uncertainty and acceptance.

Competing interpretations remain active: existential, technological displacement, epistemological, recursive-form, and hybrid.

The hybrid reading is currently the most comprehensive hypothesis, not a proven conclusion.
