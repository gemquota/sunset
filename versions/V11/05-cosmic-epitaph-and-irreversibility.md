# 05 — Cosmic Epitaph and Irreversibility

If humanity disappears while its records survive, the archive can become a **cosmic epitaph**. An epitaph records that someone existed; it does not make the person present.

The same distinction applies to technological inheritance. A future machine could become an exceptionally capable reader of humanity's surviving records without becoming the people commemorated by those records.

Irreversibility adds a historical dimension. Once capabilities, infrastructure, and knowledge exist, they can alter the future possibility-space. Rewinding one decision does not necessarily restore the previous world.
