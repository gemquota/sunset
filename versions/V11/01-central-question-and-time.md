# 01 — Central Question and Time

## The central question

**Will the computer love the sunset?** is not treated as a prediction requiring a definite answer. Its force comes from changing the identity of the possible interpreter.

A future machine could encounter human representations without sharing the experiences that produced them. That makes the relationship between experience, representation, and interpretation technologically consequential.

## Nested time

The refrain can be read as inviting three nested scales:

1. biological/evolutionary time;
2. human/technological time;
3. possible machine or post-human time.

The third is a possibility, not an established future stage. The layers are nested and historically connected, but the song does not establish a deterministic sequence from evolution to technology to AI to extinction.
