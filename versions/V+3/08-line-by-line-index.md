## Repetition as evidence

The repeated chorus lines are not redundant entries. Their recurrence is itself evidence for the argument about form. The same verbal material returns after different contextual information has accumulated, allowing the listener's interpretation to change without the underlying words changing.

That is the strongest concrete bridge between the song's **repetition**, its **recursive interpretive effect**, and the project's broader claim that later context can alter the meaning available in earlier material.
