# 02 — Form and Recursive Listener

The song's form can be said to **enact** aspects of its subject through repetition, temporal nesting, and retrospective reinterpretation.

The listener's recursive process is:

```text
hear → interpret → encounter new context → modify interpretation
→ return to earlier material → discover new relationships → interpret again
```

One interpretive result becomes context for the next pass. The song does not change between readings; the listener's representation of it does.

This supports a cognition-level recursive interpretation without claiming that listening is literally equivalent to machine recursive self-improvement.
