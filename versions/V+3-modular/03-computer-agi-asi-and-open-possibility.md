# 03 — Computer, AGI, ASI, and Open Possibility

The computer matters because it can be read as a possible **different kind of interpreter**.

The essay should use **AI / machine intelligence** as the broad category. AGI and ASI are narrower hypothetical possibilities, not assumed endpoints.

The interpretive sequence is:

> **possibility → conceptual consequence → not prediction**

The possibility matters because a sufficiently capable machine could become an interpreter of human representations at a scale or capability level that changes what those representations mean socially and historically.

This opens questions about inheritance, interpretation, subjectivity, continuity, agency, and irreversibility. None requires an extinction ending.

The open ending is therefore part of the song's force. The question is what happens if the future interpreter is no longer human, not what exact future technology the song predicts.
