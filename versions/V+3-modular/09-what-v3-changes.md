# 09 — What V+3 Changes

V+3 is both conceptual and editorial.

## Conceptual

- AGI and ASI are no longer assumed endpoints.
- AI/machine intelligence is the broad category.
- The changed future interpreter is the central technological possibility.
- Possibility, consequence, and prediction are explicitly separated.
- The open ending is treated as meaningful.
- RSI remains a structural analogy, not a forecast.

## Editorial

- The canonical essay receives a table of contents and concrete conceptual organization.
- A modular edition provides smaller reading units.
- V+1 and V+2 receive modular reader maps.
- A lyric-by-lyric index provides concise interpretations and essay links without reproducing the complete copyrighted lyrics.

## Core claim

> **The song asks what happens to the meaning of human experience when the possible future interpreter of humanity is no longer human.**
