# 04 — Representation, Experience, and Our Story

"Our story" becomes increasingly unstable as later material is considered.

It can mean the story people lived, humanity as a species, the accumulated record of human history, or a later intelligence's reconstruction of those records.

The central asymmetry is:

> **A representation can survive the experience that produced it.**

Language, images, recordings, databases, code, art, and radio signals can preserve traces without preserving the embodied subject. This is why the sunset question is also a question about whether interpretation of an experience can ever become the experience itself.
