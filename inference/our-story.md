# “Our story”

**Confidence:** Low to Moderate

“Our story” is treated as a deliberately unstable pronoun. It may refer to humanity, the narrator and another person, the species across history, the cultural record, or a possible successor's reconstruction of that record.

The ambiguity is interpretive rather than a confirmed statement of authorial intent. Its value lies in how later AI and archive themes change the listener's sense of who is included in “our.”
