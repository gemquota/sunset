# Structural resonance

**Confidence:** Moderate when the formal correspondence is explicit; Low when the correspondence depends on broad thematic similarity.

Structural resonance identifies a comparable formal relationship between two works without claiming historical influence or authorial intention.

The *Infinite Jest* layer uses this category for representation versus experience, closed-loop recursion, archival density, self-reference, and inheritance. The comparison must preserve mechanism differences rather than flattening them into “both are recursive.”

**Required boundary:** structural resonance is not evidence of influence.
