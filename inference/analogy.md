# Analogy

**Confidence:** Moderate as a structural comparison; low as a literal equivalence.

An analogy maps a useful relationship from one domain onto another while preserving the fact that the systems differ.

The essay uses recursive self-improvement as an analogy for recursive interpretation. The processes share a broad shape in which the current state influences later processing, but listening to a song is not equivalent to AI self-improvement.

The *Infinite Jest* comparison reinforces this boundary by distinguishing open developmental loops from closed compulsive loops.
