# Recursive self-improvement

**Confidence:** Moderate

Recursive self-improvement (RSI) is used as a conceptual analogy for a system whose current capabilities can modify or improve the capabilities that determine later processing.

The essay compares this structure with recursive interpretation, while explicitly stating that listening to a song is not equivalent to AI self-improvement.

The *Infinite Jest* comparison adds an important control distinction: recursion can be developmental or compulsive. Repetition alone is not evidence of improvement.
