# Representation versus experience

**Confidence:** High for the distinction; Moderate for its application to AI.

A representation can convey information about an experience without being identical to the experience that produced it. The essay applies this distinction to language, recordings, archives, and machine-readable traces.

The distinction does not by itself establish that an AI system lacks subjective experience. It establishes only that access to representations is not logically identical to access to the original experience.
