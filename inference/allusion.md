# Allusion and influence

**Confidence:** Unknown / disputed for any *Infinite Jest* allusion in the song.

An allusion claim concerns a work intentionally referring to another work. An influence claim concerns a historical relationship in which one work or creator affected another.

The repository currently has no sufficient evidence to treat the song's “infinite jest” wording as a confirmed Wallace allusion. If the project later makes that claim, it should cite the relevant lyric and provide independent evidence rather than inferring intent from resemblance alone.

Until then, *Infinite Jest* belongs in the project as structural or thematic resonance, not as established influence.
