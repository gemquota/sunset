# Clarification note

This note records the clarification prompted by the user's question about “imported assumptions” in Q17.

An imported assumption occurs when concepts from modern AI discourse are used to make claims that the song itself does not support. The project can safely infer recursive interpretation from the act of rereading and then use recursive self-improvement as a structural analogy. It should not leap from that analogy to claims about a specific AGI architecture, inevitable recursive takeoff, or a prediction about the future.

The useful distinction is:

**recursive interpretation → recursive refinement → structural analogy to self-improvement → technological framing through “The Computer”**

The analogy becomes problematic only when the direction is reversed and AI theory is used to manufacture evidence for the interpretation.
